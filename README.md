# Second-Serve
[AI-driven circular food redistribution. Connecting surplus to need.]

Second Serve is an artificial intelligence-based circular food redistribution solution that aims to decrease the amount of food waste in cities and solve food insecurity. Through the platform, restaurants can post any extra food, and the machine learning models would then learn when the food is safe to consume, and then the nearest NGOs and beneficiaries would be matched with the food.

When food is not taken, it will automatically be reused as an animal feed, then compost, so that food waste does not become not used at all.

The combination of AI and sustainability and social welfare will help Second Semver to develop a scalable, data-driven approach to smarter urban resources management.
